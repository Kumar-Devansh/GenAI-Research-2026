The Indian Southwest Monsoon typically sets over Kerala around 1 June and covers Delhi by late June to early July.

Monsoon withdrawal from northwest India usually begins around 17 September.

Heat waves in Delhi occur when maximum temperature crosses 40 C and departs 4.5 C or more from normal.

Heavy rainfall is 64.5 to 115.5 mm in 24 hours; very heavy is 115.6 to 204.4 mm.
